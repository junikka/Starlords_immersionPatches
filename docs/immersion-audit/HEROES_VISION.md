# Vision: Heroes, a Sector that lives

**Status: vision and design for Julie's review (2026-10-24).** Nothing is implemented. This widens the mod's premise.

## The idea

Star Lords made a faction's commanders into characters with fleets, holdings and politics. The expansion makes **any interesting character** a traveller with a life of their own: a ruler leading a war fleet, a smuggler on a run to Kanta's Den, a famous explorer heading past the Fringe, a preacher on pilgrimage, a mercenary for hire, a DJ touring the raves. The player meets them out in the Sector and can deal with them in many ways. They are never killed, because many are lore characters and should stay as vanilla as vanilla.

> *If this were Star Wars, you'd meet Han Solo on a smuggling run, or Luke flying some mission you can get involved in.* And you'd watch Philip Andrada's fleet decimating someone.

**A Hero** is a character the game tracks individually: a person, a role, an agenda, and a presence in space. A fleet can be one ship with one person aboard. Star Lords' commanders become one kind of hero, the political-military kind, alongside many others.

## What a hero is made of

| Part | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Identity** | A person: an existing lore character (same person object, never a copy) or an original one, with portrait, biography and personality | Philip Andrada; "Vex Calloway", an original smuggler |
| **Archetype** | What kind of life they lead: decides their agendas, the interactions they offer and the dialogue sets they use | Ruler, Commander, Smuggler, Trader, Explorer, Mercenary, Courier, Pilgrim, Diplomat, Celebrity |
| **Agenda** | What they're doing right now, generated by the archetype: a destination, a purpose and a duration, shown on their fleet ("Smuggling to Kanta's Den", "Surveying beyond Magec") | |
| **Presence** | How they exist in space: a real fleet near the player, a lightweight route far away; occasionally ashore in a comm directory or bar | |
| **Ties** | Faction (optional; many heroes are independent), relationships with the player and other heroes, a home | |
| **Protection** | How far consequences can go. Lore heroes: never killed, defeat is a retreat (see the [lore research](LORE_CHARACTERS_RESEARCH.md)). Original heroes: the same by default; optionally killable through the Life and Death setting | |

## Archetypes (first proposal)

| Archetype | Typical agendas | Ways to interact |
|---|---|---|
| **Ruler** | Lead a war, defend the capital, host the grand rave | Audience, council favours, join their campaign, be knighted… |
| **Commander** (today's Star Lords) | Campaigns, patrols, revenue, raves | Everything Star Lords already has |
| **Smuggler** | Runs between free ports and pirate dens, hiding from patrols | Buy contraband or tips, escort them, report them, bribe them for a route |
| **Trader / merchant prince** | Long trade routes between core worlds | Trade at sea, invest, escort, tip-offs on prices |
| **Explorer / scavenger** | Expeditions past the Fringe, salvage | Buy survey data, join the expedition, rescue them |
| **Mercenary** | Contracts for whoever pays | Hire them (like Nexerelin's merc companies), fight beside or against them |
| **Courier / envoy** | Diplomatic messages between capitals | Escort, intercept (with consequences), carry a reply |
| **Pilgrim / preacher** | Luddic pilgrimages to shrines | Escort, donate, debate, convert or be converted |
| **Celebrity** | Touring raves and tournaments | Turns up at raves; brings rumours, gifts, a rivalry |

## How the player interacts

The point is many small, meaningful contacts, not one menu:

- **Hail them in space:** dialogue in their archetype's voice (gonzo, from your point of view); ask what they're doing; get rumours.
- **Help or hinder their agenda:** escort, intercept, report, join. Their agenda reacts, and so does their opinion of you.
- **Trade and services:** whatever the archetype offers (survey data, contraband, hire).
- **Ashore:** some heroes can be met at markets, bars or raves when their fleet is docked.
- **Relationships:** the existing Star Lords relation system carries over: friendship, rivalry, romance where it fits.
- **Follow them:** a *Heroes* intel tab lists the heroes you know, with their last known agenda and location.

## Building on what already exists

- **Star Lords** already has persons with fleets, an AI choosing actions, relations, dialogue, prisoners, raves, intel. Commanders become the Commander archetype; the rest generalises.
- **Vanilla `RouteManager`** runs trade fleets and patrols as lightweight routes, which only become real fleets within about 1.6 light-years of the player. That lets dozens of heroes exist without dozens of always-live fleets. Today's commanders are always-live fleets, which works for 48 of them but not for a crowded Sector.
- **Vanilla `PersonalFleetScript`** proves the pattern of lore characters commanding real fleets (Hyder, Caden), with respawn after defeat.
- **Vanilla contacts and missions** provide the job-giving pattern. **Nexerelin's mercenary companies** provide the hire pattern.
- **The lore research's guards** apply to every lore hero:
  - the same person object;
  - the comm board, post and faction untouched;
  - stepping aside while `$missionImportant`;
  - never doubling a character vanilla already fields;
  - Nexerelin-safe handling of their home market.

## Architecture in short

1. **Hero registry:** data packs keyed by stable IDs (`data/lords/heroes/*.json`), one entry per hero: identity (lore person ID or original), archetype, home, faction, protection, dialogue overrides. This is Phase 5's "commander packs" widened, including adding heroes to a running campaign and remembering the dead.
2. **Archetype definitions** (data plus a small Java class each): the agenda generator, interactions, dialogue sets and fleet template.
3. **Agenda engine:** picks an agenda, plans a route, updates it as days pass, and reacts to the player and events. Commanders keep today's LordAI; other archetypes get simple state machines.
4. **Presence layer:** route-based for most heroes, always-live for commanders and rulers where the strategic AI needs it.
5. **Interaction layer:** the existing dialogue engine with archetype dialogue sets and new addons (trade, hire, escort, intercept).
6. **Intel:** the commander intel generalised into a Heroes tab.

## Roadmap

| Step | Content |
|---|---|
| **H0** | Internal groundwork: a *Hero* abstraction above today's `Lord` (role/archetype, protection, presence), with no visible change. Stable IDs. |
| **H1** | **Rulers take the field:** Andrada, Daud, Sun and Hannan, fully protected (the approved backup plan, as the Ruler archetype). |
| **H2** | **First non-military archetype**, end to end: Smuggler or Trader, two or three original heroes, route-based presence, agendas visible on the map, a Heroes intel tab, hailing and two or three interactions. |
| **H3** | More archetypes (Explorer, Mercenary, Courier, Pilgrim, Celebrity) and more interactions (escort, hire, intercept, meet ashore or at raves). |
| **H4** | Hero packs for modders and other faction mods: manifests, validation, adding heroes mid-campaign (Phase 5 folded in). Lore heroes beyond rulers, where the lore research allows. |

Phases 4 (faction dialogue) and 5 (packs) of the original design fold into this: dialogue becomes per archetype and faction, and packs become hero packs.
